# PRIVACY POLICY — ANATOMIFY

**Last updated:** July 10, 2026

## 1. Who we are

Anatomify is an educational anatomy application operated by **Ruben Correia de Araújo** and **Augusto da Rocha Massa**, individuals based in Recife, Pernambuco, Brazil. In this Policy, "we", "our", and "Anatomify" refer to both of us.

For any question or request related to your data, you can contact us at **hello@anatomify.app**.

## 2. Purpose of this policy

This Privacy Policy clearly explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, with whom we share it, and what your rights are as the owner of that data. It applies to all users of the Anatomify application.

We comply with the **Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law No. 13,709/2018)**, and for users outside Brazil, we respect equivalent principles from the **GDPR** (Europe) and similar local regulations.

## 3. What data we collect

**3.1. Sign-up data:**
- Name
- Email
- Password (stored in an encrypted way, never in readable text)

If you choose to sign in via Google or Apple, we receive only the email and, when available, the name. We do not have access to your Google/Apple password or to any other data from those accounts.

**3.2. Onboarding profile data:**
- Country of residence
- University or school
- How you heard about Anatomify
- Study self-assessment
- Anatomical system currently being studied
- Result of the initial quick test (trivia)

**3.3. Usage data during the use of the application:**
- Accumulated XP, module progress, and personal records
- Flashcard results (correct answers, mistakes, and accuracy)
- Study sessions (date, duration, structures reviewed)
- Error history (structures with recurring difficulty)
- Total time using the app

**3.4. Technical data:**
- Device type and operating system
- App language
- Anonymous session identifiers

**3.5. Data you can voluntarily provide:**
- Profile picture (if you choose to add one via library or camera)
- Testimonials, app ratings, and feedback

**We do not collect sensitive data** (race, sexual orientation, health, political opinion, biometric data, etc.), except for the study data itself (which relates to the educational process, not to your health).

## 4. Why we collect this data (purposes)

- **Sign-up data**: to create and protect your account.
- **Onboarding data**: to personalize your initial experience and generate your study plan.
- **Usage data**: to track your progress, generate records, update your XP, display history, and enable features such as groups and rankings.
- **Technical data**: to improve the stability and performance of the application.
- **Photo and testimonials**: only for display in your profile or in the application, with your consent.

**Legal bases (LGPD, arts. 7 and 11):**
- Performance of a contract with you (use of the service)
- Consent (when you check the acceptance boxes)
- Legitimate interest (to improve the app and prevent fraud)
- Compliance with legal obligations (when applicable)

## 5. With whom we share your data

We use the following third-party services, all with adequate security policies:

- **Supabase Inc.** — database storage and authentication. Servers may be located in the United States, the European Union, or other regions.
- **Google LLC** — for "Continue with Google" login.
- **Apple Inc.** — for "Continue with Apple" login.

We do not sell, rent, or exchange your data with advertisers or commercial partners.

In the future, we may integrate analytics tools (such as Sentry or PostHog) to monitor errors and app usage. If that happens, we will update this Policy beforehand.

We may be required to share data with public authorities in case of a court order or legal determination, always within the limits of the law.

## 6. International data transfer

Since Supabase may operate servers outside Brazil, your data may be transferred to other countries. We do this based on consent and on the contractual safeguards offered by Supabase, in compliance with article 33 of the LGPD.

## 7. How long we keep your data

- While your account is active, we keep all your profile, usage, and history data.
- If you **delete your account**, all data directly linked to your profile (name, email, XP, sessions, photo, etc.) is removed from our systems within **30 days**.
- Some anonymized data (aggregated statistics) may be kept indefinitely for internal analysis, since it does not allow your identification.

## 8. Your rights (LGPD, art. 18)

As the owner of your data, you have the right, at any time, to:

1. Confirm what data we hold about you
2. Access that data
3. Correct incomplete, inaccurate, or outdated data
4. Request anonymization, blocking, or deletion of unnecessary data
5. Data portability
6. Delete data processed based on consent
7. Revoke your consent
8. Be informed about with whom we share your data

You can exercise all these rights:
- **Inside the app**, on the Profile screen, you'll find the option "Delete my account and all data".
- **By email**, writing to **hello@anatomify.app**.

We will respond to your request within 15 days.

## 9. Data security

We adopt technical and organizational measures to protect your data:
- Passwords stored with cryptographic hashing
- Communication with our servers via HTTPS
- Access restricted to Ruben and Augusto only
- Regular backups in secure environments

No system is 100% invulnerable. In case of a security incident that may affect your data, we will notify you and the ANPD (Brazilian Data Protection Authority) as required by the LGPD.

## 10. Email communications

We may send you emails to:
- Confirm your registration, reset password, or notify you about account activity
- Communicate changes to this Policy or the Terms
- Send news, tips, and promotions related to Anatomify (marketing)

You can **unsubscribe from marketing emails** at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in those emails, without affecting your account's operation.

## 11. Device permissions

Anatomify requests the following permissions:
- **Push notifications**: for study reminders and news
- **Photo library**: for you to choose a profile picture
- **Camera**: for you to take a profile picture
- **App rating**: to ask for a rating on the App Store/Google Play

You can revoke any of these permissions in your device settings at any time.

## 12. Minors

Anatomify is intended for users aged **13 or older**. If you are between 13 and 18 years old, you must use the application with the consent of your parents or legal guardians.

We do not intentionally collect data from children under 13. If we identify or are notified of such collection, we will delete the data immediately. If you are a guardian of a child under 13 who created an account, please contact hello@anatomify.app.

## 13. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy occasionally. Whenever this happens, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of the document. If the changes are significant, we will notify you in the app or by email.

We recommend you review this Policy periodically.
