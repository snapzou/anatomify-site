# Anatomify Support

Anatomify is a gamified anatomy study app for medical students and healthcare learners, providing interactive modules across all body systems.

## Frequently Asked Questions

## How do I sign in?

You can sign in with Apple ID or email. Your progress syncs across devices when you're logged in with the same account.

## How do I subscribe to Anatomify+?

Tap any locked module and the paywall will appear with 3 plans (monthly, 6-month, annual). The 6-month plan includes 3 days free trial, the annual plan includes 7 days free trial.

## How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Anatomify+ → Cancel Subscription. You'll keep access until the end of the current billing period.

## How do I restore purchases on a new device?

Open the paywall in the app and tap "Restore purchases". Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

## What's included in the free version?

The Skull subsystem (skeletal system), Anterior Face module (muscular system), and General subsystem (nervous system). All other content requires Anatomify+.

## How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account inside the app. This permanently removes your data.
